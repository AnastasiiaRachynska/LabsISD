SPEC-GATE: Software Requirements Specification creation

The baseline version of the Software Requirements Specification (SRS) for the mobile idea organizer system was approved and files spec/srs.tex and traceability-matrix.tex were created.

The following tasks were completed:
- Conducted a structured SKED dialogue, the summary of which is recorded in logs/sked-summary.md;
- The specification contains functional and non-functional requirements with identifiers and priorities, acceptance criteria, BDD scenarios for key behavioral requirements, and a fully populated traceability matrix;
- Executed a quality audit of the specification that identified and resolved key defects: subjective formulations ("meaningful labels", "normal operating conditions"), unclear Jest coverage configuration (>= 85%), undefined log rotation parameters (ISO-8601 UTC, 2 MB FIFO), the Display Title generation rule (25 characters + ...), Chapter sorting order, and missing entries in the traceability matrix;
- Established strict AND logic for multi-filters.
