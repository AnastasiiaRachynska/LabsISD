The idea is to create an information system for organizing a writer's creative ideas. Users can create individual books and add various entries to them—such as characters, scenes, phrases and dialogue, plot ideas, world-building details, and other creative notes. It should also allow for saving general ideas that aren't yet tied to a specific book. In essence, the goal is to enable writers to quickly capture specific details, concepts, and creative ideas, while also making it easy to structure and retrieve them later. This approach aims to offer a more convenient solution than standard note-taking apps, specifically for managing a large volume of diverse creative ideas.
